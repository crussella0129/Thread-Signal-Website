# Media sources

Updated October 23, 2026.

## Yardulum app icon
`src/assets/yardulum-icon.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of `apps/field/public/icons/icon-192.png` in Charles's Yardulum repository (md5 15cde08ac0dc86c21a415a61101c70c7). It is the product's own icon (survey stake, pink flag, yellow tape on green), by Thread & Signal. It is also embedded in `public/social-card-yardulum.svg`. Do not redraw it.

## Yardulum illustrations
The home figure and the How It Works figures are hand-coded SVG and HTML diagrams of the documented sales loop (property model, design, price, proposal). They use a synthetic sample yard, not a real address, and sample prices from `src/data/sample-job.ts`. They are labelled "Illustration" and "Sample prices". They are not screenshots, quotes or customer work, and they do not stand in for a real build. No real property, including the Denver test samples and county imagery, appears on the site.

## Logo
The Signalium Thread & Signal lockup in `src/assets/logo/` and in the social card is unchanged.

## Retired
The Jetson Orin Nano Super Case photo and CAD render left the site with the old home page and were deleted from `src/assets/`. The project remains on GitHub and in the Open-Source ranking.
