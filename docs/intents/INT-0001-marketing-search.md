## Intent
Help prospective clients understand and commission Thread & Signal's AI automation, local LLM deployment, agent workshops, and CAD work. Retain the approved Lamplight Atelier identity and direct-email funnel. Ground claims in Charles Russella's existing public work and Ohio identity. Upgrade the repository to the current Sprint Loops substrate, publish the completed sprint through the existing GitHub Pages pipeline, and verify the live site.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The homepage identifies Charles and explains the automation/local AI offer; services and contact provide a concrete workflow brief without invented prices, testimonials, guarantees, or credentials.
2. A substantive local LLM feasibility guide is linked from commercial pages, names its author, and leads to the existing email/contact funnel.
3. Indexable pages have distinct descriptive metadata, production canonicals, valid matching structured data, a working social image, and sitemap coverage without redirect aliases.
4. Build, Astro type checks, relevant regression/output checks, desktop/mobile navigation and lead paths pass. The tested change reaches main through one sprint PR and the live custom domain serves the new content.
5. Sprint Loops 0.22.0 is verified against upstream; losslessly migrated history, contract-4 substrate, intent/task records, and phase evidence are tracked.

## Rationale
The public Rust/Python, Animus, Sprint Loops, and hardware projects provide concrete differentiation. Buyer language, helpful content, clear attribution, and an easier first contact improve the site's usefulness and crawlable context.

## Alternatives
A visual rebrand would discard an approved identity. Keyword-heavy doorway pages and fabricated client proof would weaken trust. Paid ads, profile edits, new hosting, analytics accounts, and Search Console ownership changes are outside this sprint.

## Consequences
Search indexing and ranking remain external outcomes; deployment can be verified immediately. Maintain the new guide when tools change. Keep snapshot metrics dated and avoid claiming special AI ranking benefits from llms.txt. Existing hosting and email remain in use.
