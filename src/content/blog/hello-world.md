Welcome to the Thread & Signal blog. This is the workshop journal: technical
deep-dives, project updates, and lessons learned from building automation,
agents, and physical designs — by hand, on hardware we own.

## What we're working on

The center of the bench is the **Animus project** — an open-source agentic
coding harness for small local models (1B–14B GGUF), written in Rust — plus a
line of harness-agnostic agent skills (Sprint Loops, GECK, MDR) and a steady
stream of CAD and 3D-printing work. We'll be documenting all of it here.

## What to expect

- Local-first AI: agents and LLM deployments that never leave the building
- Agent skills and loop engineering across Claude Code, Codex, and open harnesses
- CAD, 3D printing, and design-for-manufacturing notes
- General software craft, mostly in Rust and Python

Stay tuned. The loom is warmed up.
